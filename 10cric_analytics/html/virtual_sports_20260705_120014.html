FAQ

What Are Virtual Sports?

Virtual sports are computer-generated events that function like supercharged video games. Through cutting-edge algorithms and software, they recreate real cricket, football, tennis, racing, and other sports in the digital world, adding a dash of unpredictability for good measure.

Virtual sports are designed to resemble genuine sports betting as closely as possible. Give them a try, and you’ll enjoy high-definition simulations of arenas, fields, and pitches with cheering fans, blinding lights, commentators, and fantasy teams. Tournament and league formats add a competitive zing, and RNG technology ensures fair outcomes. With diverse markets and competitive odds, it’s truly a betting adventure like no other.

Is there a schedule for virtual sports events, or are they available at any time?

Virtual sports provide on-demand entertainment. You won’t have to check a schedule or wait for days to bet. The 10CRIC website and app offer access to continuous virtual cricket, football, basketball, and tennis action.

How are odds determined in virtual sports betting?

Virtual sports rely on RNG technology to replicate the unpredictability of real sporting events and attach appropriate odds to each party in the fixture.

Are virtual sports betting outcomes purely random, or do they follow a specific algorithm?

To determine the outcomes of their matches, virtual sports developers employ algorithmic models based on various factors. They take perceived team/player performance, historical statistics, pitch conditions, and many other similar aspects into account, in addition to an element of chance.

Are there specific rules for virtual sports betting that differ from traditional sports betting?

Not really. Virtual sports betting works similarly to standard sports betting. You browse the fixture, choose the most enticing markets and odds, and add your choices to the betting slip.

Can I place live bets on virtual sports events at 10CRIC?
